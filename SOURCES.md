# Sources and verification notes

Snapshot prepared November 22, 2026. Dates were checked against enacted public laws; the Congressional Research Service supplies historical context and the definition of completion.

## Method and context

- [CRS R47235: The Congressional Budget Process Timeline](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47235), especially “Final Enactment of Regular Appropriations.” Identifies FY1997 as the last on-time year.
- [CRS R46595: Continuing Resolutions: Overview of Components and Practices](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R46595). Distinguishes regular appropriations, interim continuing resolutions, and full-year continuing resolutions. Its interim-CR expiration dates are not necessarily enactment dates; this dataset uses enactment dates.

## Special cases

- **FY1997:** completion was September 30, 1996, one day before October 1. The signed elapsed-day value is −1; the chart shows zero days late.
- **FY2004:** the HTML law header incorrectly says 2003. [GovInfo metadata](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-108publ199) and [Congress.gov actions](https://www.congress.gov/bill/108th-congress/house-bill/2673/all-actions) establish January 23, 2004.
- **FY2007, FY2011, FY2013, FY2025:** completion used a full-year continuing resolution for some or all regular appropriations.
- **FY2020:** Public Laws 116-93 and 116-94 were both enacted December 20, 2019; the dataset links 116-94.
- **FY2026:** April 30 is the enactment of the last regular appropriations measure, [Public Law 119-86](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ86/html/PLAW-119publ86.htm). Specified immigration-enforcement funding was handled separately in [Public Law 119-98](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ98/html/PLAW-119publ98.htm), enacted June 10, 2026. April 30 is not a claim that every funding dispute was resolved on that date. Using June 10 changes the FY2026 value from 211 to 252 days.

## Rechecking a row

Follow its source link and confirm the approval date and fiscal year. Then consult the relevant CRS appropriations history to establish that the measure completed the regular process; a law’s date alone does not establish that it was the last measure. Record any correction in the CSV notes and update this file.
